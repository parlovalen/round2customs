VPIN NOIRE

A contemporary take on the classic pinball machine, featuring a clean, furniture-inspired silhouette. Packed with immersive digital gameplay, premium materials, and cinematic visuals, it's designed to be the centerpiece of any room.

After discovering us at the Midwest Gaming Classic, our client envisioned transforming the mezzanine of his garage condo into a space where classic gaming, great company, and a love of cars all came together.

DESIGN

What sets this machine apart is the woodgrain finish and pedestal base that create a modern furniture aesthetic that's visually appealing and extremely sturdy.

THE BUILD PROCESS

  • Design. Sketches and CAD elevations worked out flipper height against armrest angle, and the brass inlay on the backbox surround went through four width iterations before we cut a single panel.

  • Manufacturing. Every panel is CNC-cut from cabinet-grade Baltic birch off the same digital file, so tolerances stay identical from the first part to the thirty-fourth.

  • Wiring. The PC, force-feedback board, displays, speakers, and LED controller all tie back to a single labeled harness, so anything inside can be serviced without disturbing anything else.

  • Assembly. Sides are clamped and glued square before a single fastener goes in, edges are banded and trimmed flush by hand, and the finish is sanded back between every coat.

  • Delivery. Every table is tested end to end, the cabinet is crated, and it arrives set up and playable — levelled on its feet and ready for its first ball.
