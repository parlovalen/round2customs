Overview

A brand that needed to match the quality of the work behind it.

EW came in with a strong product and no visual identity to show for it. The existing brand wasn't communicating the craft behind what they were building, and the website wasn't converting the interest they were generating.

The brief was clear: build a brand worth standing behind and an online store built to sell — starting from the mark and carrying it all the way through to checkout.

Brand Strategy

Before designing anything, we mapped out what EW needed to stand for.

We opened with a brand values exercise to define what the identity needed to carry. A mind-mapping session surfaced visual symbols and associations that aligned with how the team wanted to be perceived — and just as importantly, clarified what to leave out.

Logo Design

Concepting until the mark could carry the brand on its own.

Multiple directions explored different visual territories — wordmarks, monograms, icon-based marks — before narrowing to the concept that felt right. The refinement process sharpened the geometry and tested the mark across sizes and contexts before locking in the final.

Brand System

Color, typography, and tone working as one.

The palette was built for versatility — readable across digital and print, with enough warmth to feel approachable without losing credibility. Typography pairs a geometric display face with a clean workhorse for body copy and UI, establishing a tone that reads as considered without being overdesigned.

E-commerce Design

The website needed to match the brand — and outperform what was there before.

The existing site had no clear hierarchy and buried products behind navigation that didn't reflect how customers actually shopped. We started with a sitemap to define the information architecture, moved into wireframes, then applied the brand system to every surface before handing off.

Design System

Every UI element built to be reused.

A component library covering product cards, navigation, forms, search, and interactive states gave the development team a stable foundation to build from and extend. The system scales naturally as the product catalog grows.

How it comes together

Brand and product, working as a unified system.

The final site carries the new brand all the way through — from landing page to checkout. Mobile received the same design rigor as desktop. The result is an experience that earns the trust of a customer before they've read a single product description.
