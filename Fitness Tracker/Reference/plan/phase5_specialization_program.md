# Phase 5: Specialization (Weeks 33–40)

> By this point you'll have 8 monthly assessments of data (Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32). This phase is built as a **choose-your-focus framework** rather than a fixed prescription, because "what's lagging" is something your actual assessment photos/measurements/strength log will tell you — not something I can predict in advance.

---

## Phase Goals
- Core 4-day Upper/Lower structure continues unchanged — main lifts stay in a hypertrophy-strength blend (6-10 reps), maintaining what you've built
- 2 exercise slots per session are swapped for a **specialization track** targeting whatever needs the most work
- **Gate check for dynamic work:** if your Week 32 (or later) assessment shows sustained clean knee status, this phase is the earliest point to reintroduce light lateral movement, controlled deceleration, and fuller-range squat/lunge work — all of which happens inside Track C below
- Deload + assessment checkpoints at **Weeks 36 and 40**

## Choosing Your Track
Look at your Week 32 assessment and pick based on what actually shows up:
- **Track A — Arms & Shoulders:** photos show upper arms/shoulders lagging relative to chest/back, or you just want more definition there
- **Track B — Posterior Chain & Glutes:** measurements/photos show glutes/hamstrings lagging relative to quads/upper body
- **Track C — Athletic Performance / Soccer Carryover:** knee has been consistently clean, strength is solid, and you want the program to start paying dividends on the field — change of direction, single-leg stability, and controlled deceleration all transfer directly to soccer

You can also split the phase — 4 weeks on one track, 4 weeks on another — if two things need attention.

---

## Core Structure (all tracks — same for every session)

*Warm-up, mobility, activation: same as previous phases.*

**Lower A / Lower B and Upper A / Upper B** keep the same 4-5 main/accessory lifts from Phase 4 (RDL, Box or Front Squat, Hip Thrust, Bench Press, OHP, Rows, Pulldowns) at **4x8-10, RPE 7**, tempo and rest as in Phase 4. What changes is the **last 2 exercise slots per session**, replaced by your chosen track below.

## Progression Rule (Weeks 33–40)
- **Weeks 33-35:** Standard progression on core lifts (+5% when top of rep range hit at RPE ≤7); specialization exercises start conservative (RPE 6-7) since they're newer movement patterns
- **Week 36 — Deload + Assessment #9:** Standard structure
- **Weeks 37-39:** Continue progression on both core and specialization lifts
- **Week 40 — Deload + Assessment #10:** Standard structure; also the checkpoint for whether Advanced Growth (Phase 6) is appropriate, or whether another specialization block would serve you better first

## Track A: Arms & Shoulders

Add these 2 exercises to the end of **both** Upper A and Upper B (in place of 2 of the existing accessory slots):

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Tempo | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Close-Grip Bench Press or DB Skull Crusher | 3x10-12 | 2-1-2-0 | 75s | 7 | Triceps emphasis |
| Incline DB Curl | 3x10-12 | 2-1-2-0 | 60s | 7 | Full stretch bicep emphasis |

**Coaching cues:** Close-grip bench — elbows tucked closer than standard bench, ~shoulder-width grip. Incline curl — let the arm hang fully behind the torso at the bottom for a full stretch.
**Video search keywords:** "close grip bench press form", "incline dumbbell curl form"
**Common mistakes:** Close-grip bench: elbows flaring out (defeats the purpose). Incline curl: swinging, not using full range.

## Track B: Posterior Chain & Glutes

Add these 2 exercises to the end of **both** Lower A and Lower B:

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Tempo | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell Good Morning (light, controlled) | 3x10 | 3-1-1-0 | 90s | 6-7 | Posterior chain, very low knee stress |
| Cable/Band Standing Glute Kickback | 3x12-15/side | 2-1-2-0 | 60s | 7 | Glute isolation |

**Coaching cues:** Good morning — soft knee bend held constant, hinge from the hips, keep the bar path close to the body. Glute kickback — brace core, avoid arching the lower back to get range, squeeze glute at the top.
**Video search keywords:** "barbell good morning form beginner", "cable glute kickback form"
**Common mistakes:** Good morning: rounding the back, using too much load too soon. Kickback: using lower back instead of hip extension for range.

## Track C: Athletic Performance / Soccer Carryover

Add these 2 exercises to the end of **both** Lower A and Lower B. **Only proceed with this track if the knee has been consistently clean per the gate check above.**

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Tempo | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lateral Band Walk (already in your warm-ups — this is a loaded, standalone version) | 3x10 steps/side | Controlled | 60s | 6-7 | Frontal-plane strength for cutting/direction changes |
| Controlled Deceleration Step (low-intensity landing mechanics drill) | 3x6/side | Controlled | 90s | 6 | See detail below |

**New exercise detail — Controlled Deceleration Step:**
- **What it is:** Step forward off a low curb or step (4-6 inches), land softly on one leg, absorb the landing by bending the knee and hip, hold for 1-2 seconds, reset. This is not a jump — it's a controlled landing mechanics drill, intentionally low-intensity, aimed at teaching your body to absorb force well before any real cutting/jumping volume is added.
- **Target muscles:** Quads, glutes, tendon/landing mechanics
- **Common mistakes:** Landing stiff-legged, knee caving inward on landing
- **Coaching cues:** "Land quiet," knee tracking over toes, absorb through a soft bend rather than stopping abruptly
- **Video search keywords:** "single leg landing mechanics drill low intensity"
- **Beginner modification:** Step down from an even lower height, or land on both feet instead of one
- **Advanced progression:** Slightly higher step, add a small forward hop before the landing (only after weeks of clean landings)

**Important:** if any soreness or knee discomfort shows up after adding these, drop back to Track A or B for the remainder of the phase and revisit athletic work in Phase 6 or later.
