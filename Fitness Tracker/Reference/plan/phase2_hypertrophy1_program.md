# Phase 2: Hypertrophy Phase 1 (Weeks 5–12)

> Builds directly on Phase 1. Coaching cues, common mistakes, and video search keywords for carried-over exercises are the same as in the Phase 1 document unless noted — this file focuses on what's new and what's progressing.

---

## Phase Goals
- Increase training volume moderately now that movement patterns are established
- Add exercise variety to keep driving adaptation and reduce staleness
- **Gate check before progressing squat depth/ROM:** only increase depth on Box Squat / Goblet Squat if your Week 4 assessment showed knee pain consistently ≤2/10 during training *and* no next-morning soreness for 2+ consecutive weeks. If that bar isn't met yet, keep current depth through all of Phase 2 and reassess at Week 8.
- Continue the daily/near-daily patellar tendon isometric protocol (Spanish squat or wall sit) — this doesn't pause for any phase.
- Two built-in deload + monthly assessment checkpoints: **Week 8** and **Week 12**.

## Progression Rule (Weeks 5–12)
- **Week 5:** Return to RPE 7, at higher volume than Phase 1 (main lifts move from 3 sets → 4 sets; accessories move to 3x10-15).
- **Week 6:** If Week 5 hit top of rep range at RPE ≤7 with no knee flare, add ~5% load on main lifts.
- **Week 7:** Repeat the same progression logic. RPE 7-8.
- **Week 8 — Deload + Assessment #2:** Cut sets by ~40% (e.g., 4 sets → 2-3), keep load the same or drop slightly, RPE 5-6. Take photos/measurements, review the last month's strength log, and do a knee status check (see gate check above).
- **Week 9:** Resume from your Week 7 numbers, add ~5%.
- **Week 10-11:** Continue standard progression.
- **Week 12 — Deload + Assessment #3:** Same structure as Week 8. This is also the checkpoint for whether Phase 3's heavier, lower-rep work is appropriate given how the knee has responded.

**New exercise detail — Bulgarian Split Squat:**
- **Target muscles:** Quads, glutes, hip stabilizers
- **Common mistakes:** Front knee traveling too far past toes, torso collapsing forward, rear foot elevated too high increasing knee flexion demand
- **Coaching cues:** "Most of your weight through the front heel"; keep torso tall; start with a low bench/step (6-8") for the rear foot, not a full bench
- **Video search keywords:** "Bulgarian split squat form beginner low knee stress"
- **Beginner modification:** No rear elevation at all (reverse lunge instead), shallower depth
- **Advanced progression:** Add dumbbells, increase depth as tolerated, raise rear foot height

**Cool-down:** same as Phase 1 Lower A

**New exercise detail — Band or Stability-Ball Leg Curl:**
- **Target muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Common mistakes:** Hips sagging or lifting too high, rushing the rep
- **Coaching cues:** Keep hips elevated and level throughout; curl heels toward glutes under control
- **Video search keywords:** "stability ball hamstring curl form" or "band leg curl exercise"
- **Beginner modification:** Reduce range of motion, fewer reps
- **Advanced progression:** Single-leg variation, add ankle weight

**New exercise detail — DB Flye:**
- **Target muscles:** Chest (isolation)
- **Common mistakes:** Bending elbows too much (turns it into a press), going too heavy and losing control at the bottom
- **Coaching cues:** Slight bend in elbows held constant throughout; think "hugging a big tree," not pressing
- **Video search keywords:** "dumbbell flye proper form chest"
- **Beginner modification:** Reduce range of motion, lighter weight
- **Advanced progression:** Incline variation, slower eccentric (4-second lowering)

**Cool-down:** same as Phase 1 Upper A

**New exercise detail — Wide-Grip Pulldown/Pull-up:**
- **Target muscles:** Lats (width emphasis), biceps
- **Common mistakes:** Grip too wide (limits range and stresses shoulders), leaning back excessively
- **Coaching cues:** Grip just outside shoulder width; pull elbows down and back, chest up
- **Video search keywords:** "wide grip lat pulldown form"
- **Beginner modification:** Band-assisted, closer grip
- **Advanced progression:** Weighted pull-up, slower eccentric

## Nutrition Update for Phase 2

Same adjustment rule as before — reassess every 2 weeks against your weight trend and strength progression. Use the Week 8 and Week 12 assessments as forced checkpoints even if you haven't been tracking closely in between.

The Month 1 meal templates (Days A/B/C) still work structurally for Months 2 and 3 — just scale portions to whatever your updated calorie target is at each check-in. New recipes aren't necessary unless you want variety; say the word if you'd like a fresh rotation instead of repeating Month 1's meals.
