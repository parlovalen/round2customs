# Month 1 Meal Plan & Recipes

**Target: ~2,600 kcal | 185g protein | 80g fat | 285g carbs | 30-38g fiber**

Three rotating day templates below (A, B, C) so you're not eating identical meals every day, but still only really cooking 3-4 distinct recipes per week. Each day lands within roughly 5-10% of your targets on protein/fat, slightly under on carbs/calories by design — **use the "flex add-on" list at the end of each day to close the gap** based on hunger and how training went that day. That's intentional: real adherence beats fake precision, and a food-logging app will fine-tune the exact numbers far better than hand calculation ever will.

No allergies or restrictions to work around, so this uses common, inexpensive proteins (chicken, turkey, eggs, canned tuna, dairy) as the backbone.

## Day A

### Breakfast — Greek Yogurt Power Bowl
*~585 kcal | 50g protein | 56g carb | 19g fat | 7g fiber*

**Ingredients (1 serving):**
- 1.5 cups (340g) plain 2% Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup (40g) rolled oats
- 1 tbsp (16g) peanut butter
- 1/2 cup (75g) mixed berries

**Instructions:** Stir oats directly into cold yogurt (they soften in ~5 min) or microwave 45s if you prefer them warm. Top with peanut butter and berries.
**Prep time:** 3 min | **Cook time:** 0 (or 1 min microwaved)
**Storage:** Best fresh; oats can be pre-portioned into containers for the week.

### Lunch — Chicken & Rice Bowl
*~780 kcal | 79g protein | 77g carb | 12.5g fat | 5g fiber*

**Ingredients (1 serving, scale up for meal prep):**
- 8 oz (227g) chicken breast
- 1.5 cups cooked white rice
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or teriyaki sauce

**Instructions:** Season chicken with salt/pepper/garlic powder, bake at 400°F for 20-25 min (or pan-sear ~6 min/side) until internal temp hits 165°F. Sauté vegetables in olive oil 4-5 min. Slice chicken over rice and vegetables, drizzle with sauce.
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 20 min
**Storage:** Fridge up to 4 days in sealed containers. Freezes well up to 2 months (freeze chicken/rice separately from fresh veg if possible).

### Dinner — Beef & Vegetable Stir Fry
*~665 kcal | 42g protein | 60g carb | 23g fat | 5g fiber*

**Ingredients (1 serving):**
- 6 oz (170g) 93% lean ground beef
- 1 cup cooked white rice
- 1.5 cups stir-fry vegetable mix (carrots, broccoli, onion, snap peas)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1-2 tbsp stir-fry or teriyaki sauce, 1 clove garlic, 1 tsp ginger (optional)

**Instructions:** Brown beef in a hot pan, breaking it up, ~6-8 min; drain excess fat if desired. Remove beef, add oil and vegetables, stir-fry 5-6 min until crisp-tender. Return beef to pan, add sauce, toss 1-2 min. Serve over rice.
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 15 min
**Storage:** Fridge up to 4 days.

### Snacks / Workout Nutrition
- **Pre-workout:** 1 banana + black coffee — 105 kcal, 27g carb
- **Post-workout:** 1 scoop whey protein + 8oz 2% milk — 250 kcal, 32g protein
- **Evening snack:** 1 oz almonds (~23 almonds) — 164 kcal, 6g protein, 14g fat

**Day A total: ~2,550 kcal, 210g protein, 241g carb, 74.5g fat**
*Flex add-on if hungry or after a hard session: extra piece of fruit, extra 1/2 cup rice, or a second tbsp of peanut butter (~150-200 kcal).*

## Day B

### Breakfast — Eggs, Toast & Avocado
*~600 kcal | 45g protein | 38g carb | 32g fat | 9g fiber*

**Ingredients:**
- 3 whole eggs + 3 egg whites
- 2 slices whole wheat toast
- 1/2 avocado
- 2 slices turkey bacon

**Instructions:** Scramble eggs/whites over medium-low heat. Cook turkey bacon per package (usually 3-4 min per side in a skillet). Toast bread, mash avocado on top.
**Prep time:** 5 min | **Cook time:** 10 min
**Storage:** Best fresh — eggs don't reheat well.

### Lunch — Tuna Quinoa Salad
*~620 kcal | 60g protein | 47g carb | 19.5g fat | 7g fiber*

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (5oz each) tuna in water, drained
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 2 cups mixed greens
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, sliced cucumber
- 1 tbsp olive oil + splash of lemon juice or vinegar

**Instructions:** Cook quinoa per package (~15 min, or batch-cook for the week). Toss all ingredients together, dress just before eating.
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 0 (if quinoa pre-cooked)
**Storage:** Fridge up to 4 days; keep dressing separate until serving if meal-prepping.

### Dinner — Sheet Pan Chicken Thighs
*~605 kcal | 46g protein | 44g carb | 28.5g fat | 8g fiber*

**Ingredients:**
- 6 oz (2 medium) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 medium potato, cubed
- 1.5 cups broccoli florets
- 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika

**Instructions:** Toss potatoes and chicken in half the oil and seasoning, roast at 425°F for 15 min. Add broccoli tossed in remaining oil, roast another 12-15 min until chicken hits 165°F and potatoes are fork-tender.
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 30 min
**Storage:** Fridge up to 4 days. Scales easily to a full sheet pan for family portions.

### Snacks / Workout Nutrition
- Greek yogurt cup + small apple — 210 kcal, 17g protein
- **Pre-workout:** 2 rice cakes with honey — 140 kcal, 32g carb
- **Post-workout:** whey shake with water — 120 kcal, 24g protein
- Flex add-on: handful of peanuts or extra fruit to close out the day (~250-300 kcal)

**Day B total (with flex add-on): ~2,550-2,600 kcal, ~195g protein**

## Day C

### Breakfast — Protein Oats
*~600 kcal | 42g protein | 72g carb | 17g fat | 7g fiber*

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats cooked in 1 cup 2% milk
- 1 scoop whey protein, stirred in once oats are slightly cooled
- 1 banana, sliced
- 1 tbsp peanut butter

**Instructions:** Simmer oats in milk 5 min, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat, stir in protein powder (don't boil it), top with banana and peanut butter.
**Prep time:** 2 min | **Cook time:** 5 min

### Lunch — Turkey Chili with Rice
*~725 kcal | 52g protein | 78g carb | 19g fat | 10g fiber*

**Ingredients (makes 4-6 servings — batch cook):**
- 1.5 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 2 cans kidney or black beans, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 diced onion, 2 cloves garlic
- Chili powder, cumin, paprika to taste
- Optional: 1oz shredded light cheese per serving
- Serve with 1 cup cooked rice per serving

**Instructions:** Brown turkey with onion and garlic, ~8 min. Add beans, tomatoes, and spices. Simmer 20-25 min, stirring occasionally. Serve over rice, top with cheese if using.
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 30 min
**Storage:** Fridge up to 5 days, freezes excellently up to 3 months — this is your best batch-cook option for busy weeks.

### Dinner — Salmon, Sweet Potato & Asparagus
*~515 kcal | 39g protein | 32g carb | 24g fat | 7g fiber*

**Ingredients:**
- 6 oz salmon fillet
- 1 medium sweet potato
- 1 cup asparagus
- 1 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon

**Instructions:** Roast sweet potato (cubed) at 425°F for 20 min. Add salmon and asparagus (tossed in oil) to the same pan, roast another 12-15 min until salmon flakes easily.
**Prep time:** 8 min | **Cook time:** 20 min

### Snacks / Workout Nutrition
- Cottage cheese (1 cup) + 1 cup pineapple — 260 kcal, 26g protein
- **Pre-workout:** banana + coffee — 105 kcal
- **Post-workout:** whey shake — 120 kcal, 24g protein
- Flex add-on: extra 1/2-1 cup rice at lunch or a small handful of trail mix (~200-275 kcal) to close the day out

**Day C total (with flex add-on): ~2,550-2,625 kcal, ~190g protein**

## Budget Notes
- Chicken thighs, ground turkey, canned tuna, eggs, and cottage cheese are your cheapest high-protein anchors — build the week around these rather than salmon/steak.
- Buy rice, oats, and beans in bulk — they're pennies per serving.
- Frozen vegetables are just as nutritious as fresh and far more forgiving on waste.
- The turkey chili is your best cost-per-gram-of-protein option and scales up easily.

## Family Meals
Sheet pan chicken thighs, beef stir fry, and turkey chili all scale up directly — just multiply ingredients and adjust pan size/cook time slightly. Good options if you're cooking for more than yourself.

## Eating Out
- Prioritize a visible protein source (grilled chicken, steak, fish) and a starch, and don't stress about the exact oil/sauce — ask for dressing or sauce on the side if you want tighter control.
- One meal out doesn't wreck a week — the flex add-ons above exist precisely so an indulgent meal fits into your weekly average rather than being "off plan."
