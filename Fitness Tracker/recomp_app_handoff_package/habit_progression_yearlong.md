# Year-Long Habit Progression

> One new habit added roughly every 4 weeks, each tied to something you're already doing (habit stacking), so it doesn't compete with everything else for willpower. Trying to add more than one at a time is the most common reason habit plans fail — resist the urge to stack several at once even if you're feeling motivated.

| Month | Weeks | New Habit | Anchor It To |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-4 | Hit protein target daily; log every workout | Already the Month 1 focus |
| 2 | 5-8 | Hit daily water target (~3.75L baseline, more on soccer days) | Right after each meal, drink a full glass |
| 3 | 9-12 | Daily step goal (start at your current baseline +1,000-2,000, don't jump to a generic 10,000 if that's a big leap) | A short walk right after lunch or right after work |
| 4 | 13-16 | Consistent sleep/wake time, including weekends | Set a recurring alarm for bedtime, not just wake time |
| 5 | 17-20 | Daily mobility routine (5-10 min: ankle, hip, thoracic spine — separate from workout warm-ups) | Right after brushing teeth in the morning |
| 6 | 21-24 | Stress management practice (5-10 min breathing or quiet time, 3-4x/week) | Right before the wind-down routine starts |
| 7 | 25-28 | Weekly meal prep session (batch-cook 2-3 recipes for the week) | Sunday, paired with the weekly check-in |
| 8 | 29-32 | Proactive recovery habit (foam rolling or stretching after training, not just when sore) | Immediately after the cool-down portion of each workout |
| 9 | 33-36 | Consolidation month — no new habit. Identify whichever habit above has been weakest and give it deliberate focus | — |
| 10 | 37-40 | Soccer-specific prep: a short dynamic warm-up before matches, a short cooldown/stretch after | Right when you arrive at the field |
| 11 | 41-44 | Weekly reflection habit — a few written sentences beyond just the numbers (what's working, what isn't) | Paired with the weekly check-in, after the data fields |
| 12 | 45-48 | Consolidation month — maintain everything built, no new additions. Priority is entering Weeks 49-52 with habits solid, not overloaded | — |
| — | 49-52 | Full review of which habits actually stuck (see the Year-End Assessment) | — |

## Why This Order
- Nutrition and logging come first (Month 1) because they're the foundation the progression rules depend on
- Water and steps (Months 2-3) are low-friction and build early momentum
- Sleep consistency (Month 4) comes once training is routine enough that a bedtime habit doesn't feel like one more thing
- Mobility and stress management (Months 5-6) are added once the basics are automatic — these are the habits most likely to get skipped if introduced too early
- Meal prep and proactive recovery (Months 7-8) are "quality of life" habits that make everything else easier once introduced
- Two full consolidation months (9 and 12) are deliberate — most 12-month plans fail by continuously adding without ever just holding steady, and holding steady is what makes habits actually stick long-term

## If a Habit Isn't Sticking
Don't add the next month's habit on schedule if the current one hasn't actually stabilized — it's fine to spend 6-8 weeks on a habit instead of 4 if it needs it. The calendar above is a default sequence, not a deadline.
